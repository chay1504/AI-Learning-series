## 2. Photogrammetry explained simply
**Simple version:** you take hundreds of overlapping photos of a real object (a pillar, a statue, a whole street) from every angle. Software finds the same points across the photos, works out their 3D position by triangulation, and builds a 3D mesh with real-photo textures. The result is a "digital twin" that drops into a game. In effect, you **photocopy reality instead of sculpting it by hand**.

**How AAA studios use it:**
- **The Vanishing of Ethan Carter** (The Astronauts, Poland, 2014): widely credited with starting the photogrammetry wave in games. An academic review (DiGRA) tracks 26 games from 2014 to 2019 that used photoscanned environments after it. [V] Source: https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/download/1225/1225
- **Star Wars Battlefront** (DICE/EA, 2015) and **Battlefront II**: scanned the original film props and real locations to rebuild Star Wars worlds. [S] Source: DiGRA review; https://medium.com/@homicidalnacho/a-look-into-photogrammetry-and-video-games-71d602f51c31
- **Quixel Megascans:** a huge library of scanned real-world surfaces and objects. **Epic Games acquired Quixel in November 2019** and made **10,000+ Megascans assets free for Unreal Engine users**. [V] Source: https://www.pcgamer.com/epic-games-acquires-quixel-and-its-huge-library-of-photogrammetry-assets
- **RealityCapture:** **Epic acquired Capturing Reality in March 2021** (terms undisclosed). It is the software behind much of Megascans. [V] Source: https://techcrunch.com/2021/03/09/epic-games-buys-photogrammetry-software-maker-capturing-reality
- **Black Myth: Wukong:** Game Science scanned real Chinese temples and statues. **27 of the game's 36 real-world locations are in Shanxi**, and these drove the tourism boom covered in section 3. [V] Source: https://technode.com/2024/08/23/black-myth-wukong-sparks-tourism-boom-in-shanxi-province-after-revealing-ancient-site-inspirations/
- Epic also released **120 free photogrammetry scans of Cambodian temples** (Scans Factory, Oct 2024). This shows how Asian heritage sites are becoming game assets. [V] Source: https://www.cgchannel.com/2024/10/download-120-free-3d-scans-of-temples-for-use-in-unreal-engine

**India angle:** India has thousands of ASI-protected monuments, and almost none are scanned for games. If Aeos's Chanderi scan is real, it is the Indian version of what Wukong did with Shanxi.
