## 1. Central question and thesis

**Paradox:** GTA 6's first day (**$3.79B ≈ ₹35,600 cr**) was about **1.7×** the **entire Indian film industry's 2025 revenue (₹20,500 cr across 1,900+ films)**. Yet India's whole games market is only ~$1.5B.

**Thesis:** gaming is already the biggest entertainment industry on Earth, and the next 10 years belong to whoever builds the games, not just plays them. India has the players, the talent gap, the stories and now AI tools, but almost none of the money yet. That gap is the opportunity.

**Workforce**
- **11.1M game developers** worldwide, including hobbyists (SlashData) [S].
- Paid professionals are estimated at only a few lakh; no census exists [U].
- The US industry supports ~**2.5 lakh jobs** (ESA 2026) [V].
- Average US salary **$142K**, but **24% were laid off** in the past 2 years (GDC) [V]. ~11,000 layoffs in 2026 so far [S].

**Studio roles** (brief A §4; art is the largest department, ~30–50% [U])
- **Design:** game, level, systems/combat, narrative, UX/UI, economy designers.
- **Engineering:** gameplay, engine, graphics, tools, network and AI programmers.
- **Art:** concept, 3D environment, character, technical artist, animator, VFX, lighting, cinematics.
- **Audio:** sound designer, composer, voice.
- **Production:** producer, project manager.
- **QA:** testers, test automation.
- **Live and business:** LiveOps, data analyst, community, **localisation**, marketing, outsourcing partners.

## 5. Part 3: India (brief B + known)

**Market**
- **50+ crore gamers**, more than the US population, but **<1% of global spend** [V].
- Lumikai: **$1.5B (2025) → $3.2B (2030)**, excluding real-money games [V].
- **Online Gaming Act 2025:** real-money games banned; video games still grew ~17% [V/S].

**Jobs**
- The AVGC task force says the sector needs **20 lakh more people by 2030** [V].
- ~**15,000** game developers vs ~58 lakh software developers [S].
- ~**1.3 lakh** professionals across **1,888** companies (AIGDF, 2024) [S].

**Pay:** entry ₹3–6 LPA (Glassdoor average ~₹5.7 LPA); mid-level ₹10–20 LPA; senior ₹18–30 LPA+ [S/U]. There is an honest "₹4 LPA floor" problem [S].

**Studios and people**
- **Global studios:** Rockstar India (Bengaluru, $7.9M in 2019), EA Hyderabad, Ubisoft Pune/Mumbai.
- **Indian studios and games:** Ogre Head (*Asura*, Hyderabad), SuperGaming (*Indus*), Nodding Heads (*Raji*), Tara Gaming (*The Age of Bhaarat*).
- **IICT Mumbai:** ₹400 cr.
- **IndiaJoy:** Hyderabad, 7–13 Dec 2026.

**Aeos Games and *Unleash the Avatar*** [V unless marked]
- **The studio:** Bengaluru. **Co-founded by Varun Mayya**; his brother **Rohan Mayya is CEO**. **40 people, "completely self-funded"**.
- **The game:** a third-person soulslike action RPG based on Indian epics. Naraka (hell) leaks into the mortal world. The hero, **Vikram**, is voiced by **Johnny Yong Bosch**. Combat uses a throwable chakra and **avatar transformations**.
- **Release:** PC first (Steam/Epic); consoles "under evaluation". Planned for 2026; the date is not confirmed [U].
- **Photogrammetry:**
  - The studio credits "in-house photogrammetry, full-body mo-cap, bespoke enemy AI".
  - It says it got **ASI permission to scan an entire town, Chanderi (MP)**, and has **1,000+ scanned assets**. These are the studio's own claims [U].
  - The engine isn't confirmed, so don't name UE5.
- **A former Ghost of Tsushima developer** joined for animation [S]. Not a "co-creator".
- **Quote:** "The future of content is gaming."

**Photogrammetry, simply:** take hundreds of photos of a real object from every angle, and software rebuilds it as a 3D model with real textures. You "photocopy reality".
- *The Vanishing of Ethan Carter* (2014) popularised it in games.
- *Star Wars Battlefront* scanned real props.
- **Epic bought Quixel (2019)**, making 10,000+ Megascans free for Unreal users, and **Capturing Reality / RealityCapture (2021)** [V].

**Precedent: Black Myth: Wukong**
- Based on *Journey to the West*.
- **27 of its 36 real locations are in Shanxi**; visitors to featured sites rose **156%** after launch [V].
- **10M copies in 3 days** [V]; 30M+ reported [S].
- Ghost of Tsushima sold 13M+ copies [V], made by an *American* studio using *Japanese* culture.
- **Line:** "If we don't make games on our stories, someone else will."

| Alternative | Data | The point |
|---|---|---|
| Bollywood | ₹20,500 cr for 1,900+ films in 2025. Theatrical rose on ticket prices; **digital and satellite rights values fell 8–10%** [V] | A film earns in weeks; a hit game earns for 10+ years (GTA V) |

| Honest caveat | Salaries are stuck near a ₹4 LPA floor; ~11k industry layoffs in 2026; hits are rare | |

**Analogy:** a wedding function vs a 50-year-old biryani hotel. A wedding is one huge day and then it's over, like a film's opening. A good biryani hotel keeps serving for decades, like a game such as GTA V or Stardew.

## 7. Part 5: AI and gaming (brief C)

**Making games**
- **Adoption:** Google Cloud's survey found 87% of developers use AI agents (vendor survey, 615 devs) [V]; GDC found 40% use GenAI at work [V]. Different definitions, so show both.
- **Cost:** Morgan Stanley says AI could cut development costs by **nearly half**, ~$22B/yr in profit [V].
- **Company bets:**
  - **Square Enix:** AI to do 70% of QA by end-2027 [V].
  - **Krafton (BGMI):** "AI First", ~$70M GPU cluster, 1,000+ GPUs [V].
- **Tools:** Unity AI agent mode (May 2026), Roblox Cube 4D generation (Feb 2026) [V].

**Inside games**
- **Fortnite's AI Darth Vader** (May 2025): players made it swear [V].
- **Where Winds Meet:** LLM-driven NPCs; **2M players in its opening weekend** [V].
- **inZOI:** AI "Smart Zoi" characters; **1M copies in its first week** of Early Access [V].
- **PUBG Ally:** an AI teammate [V].

**World models:** Google's Project Genie (Jan 2026) generates playable worlds.
- Unity stock fell **24%** in a day, Roblox 13% [V].
- Google itself says Genie "is not a game engine" [V].

**Steam:** 49.9% of new games in Oct 2026 disclosed AI use [S]. In small games it's 47%, vs 14% for big games [S].

**Backlash**
- **Clair Obscur** lost its Indie Game Awards over AI placeholder textures [V].
- **Arc Raiders** used AI voices and still sold 16M+, then re-recorded many lines with humans: "A real professional actor is better than AI" [V].
- **SAG-AFTRA** went on strike for ~11 months and won AI consent rules [V].
- **Copyright:** purely AI-made work isn't copyrightable in the US [V].

**Catalyst**
- **Pieter Levels' flight sim:** first demo in ~3 hours with AI; a self-reported "$1M ARR run-rate" in 17 days, which then faded [S].
- **Reality check:** many solo AI game-makers earn little [S].
- **The lesson:** AI gives speed; fun, taste and distribution decide success.

**How to use AI as a catalyst** (synthesis):
- prototype fast;
- use AI for code and placeholders;
- keep the art and story human;
- disclose honestly;
- build an audience early;
- start on Roblox, UEFN or the web.
