## 1. Central question and thesis

**Paradox:** companies are posting record AI revenue, yet they are cutting people. AI is blamed for record layoffs, yet AI/ML hiring is up 20–32%.

**Thesis:** AI isn't deleting the IT job. It's deleting **routine tasks**, and the people who only do those tasks.
- **Who's hit hardest:** juniors and repeat-work roles (support, basic coding, testing).
- **Who's growing:** people who build with AI, deploy it for customers, or check it.
- **The next 6 months:** the window to move from the first group into the second.

Part 1 proves the shift with numbers. Part 2 gives five moves.

## 2. Part 1: the numbers

### 2.1 US / global (brief A)

**Volume**
- **Layoffs.fyi:** about **135,446** tech layoffs at 334 companies in 2026 YTD, already above all of 2025 (122,606) [S].
- **TrueUp:** about **206,877** [S]. The trackers count differently, so always name the source.

**AI as the stated reason (Challenger, US, all industries)** [V]
- AI cited in **121,401** cuts through Oct 2026, **2.2×** all of 2025 (54,836).
- Peak month May 2026: 38,579, about 40% of that month's cuts [S].
- **Twist:** Oct 2026 had only **1,265** AI-cited cuts, the lowest month of the year, and "economic conditions" overtook AI as the top reason [S/V].
- "AI washing" is real: Oxford Economics, Yale Budget Lab, and Sam Altman himself said "it's a reality for some companies" [V].
- Tech-sector cuts are up about 54% while total US cuts are down about 39% [S].

**Roles hit**
- **Stanford "Canaries":** for 22–25-year-olds in AI-exposed jobs, the employment gap grew from **13% (2025) to 19% (Aug 2026)**. Software developers and customer service are the clearest cases [V].
- **Indeed:** software dev postings are up about 15% since Claude Code launched (Feb 2025), but **71% of that growth is senior roles** [S].

**New grads**
- **NY Fed:** recent computer engineering grads have **7.8%** unemployment (2nd of 73 majors); CS is **7.0%** (5th). This is 2024 data published Feb 2026 [V/S].
- **SignalFire:** Big Tech new-grad hiring is −65% vs 2019, and **startups −76%** (2019 is a boom-year baseline) [S].

**Hiring:** jobs needing AI skills grew **69% vs 9%** for all jobs (PwC 2026) [S]. Bloomberg reports IBM plans to triple US entry-level hiring [S].

### 2.3 Indians in the US (brief C)

**Students (Open Doors 2026, 17 Nov)**
- **Indian grad students down 34%** (1,77,892 → 1,16,609) [V].
- **Indians on OPT up 27%** to 1,82,201 [V]. More are job-hunting than studying.

**H-1B**
- **FY2027 registrations −38.5%** (3,43,981 → 2,11,600) [V].
- The **weighted lottery** is in force; reportedly only **17.7%** of those selected were at entry-level (Level I) wage [S].
- **$100K H-1B fee:**
  - **Blocked by courts** (8 Jun and 30 Sep 2026), so it is not being collected.
  - It was renewed to Sep 2027 anyway, and appeals are pending [V].
  - A separate **$103,265 fee is proposed** [V].

**Laid-off workers**
- The **60-day grace period is still the law**, but DHS **proposed ending it** (11 Sep 2026); DHS's own estimate is about 65,700 workers a year affected [V/S].
- **OPT** is unchanged. A **proposed $70K/$30K fee on schools** for OPT recommendations is not in effect [V].

### 2.4 Startups, the 30–40 second segment (briefs A and B)

- **Builder.ai** (2025): a $1.5B "AI" company whose coding was largely done by hundreds of engineers in India. It collapsed [V].
- **Ola Krutrim** went from about 550 to about 150 staff [S].

## 3. Part 2: insights, the 5 moves (brief D, tied to Part 1)

| # | Move | Why (the data) | What to do in 6 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Become AI-native in your current role** | Google: about **75%** of new code is AI-generated, up from 25% in late 2024 [V]. 65.9% of developers use AI assistants [S]. AI Engineer is #1 on LinkedIn Jobs on the Rise in the US and **fastest-growing in Hyderabad** [V] | Use a coding agent daily. Learn LLM APIs, RAG and agents/MCP. Automate your own repetitive tasks first |
| 2 | **Learn the new skill stack: context engineering → loop engineering → evals** | Prompt engineer as a title is "flatlining" (Indeed) [V]. **Context engineering** (Lütke/Karpathy, Jun 2025) [V]. **Loop engineering** (Addy Osmani, 7 Jun 2026; IBM explainer): the agent tries, a checker says pass or fail, and it retries until done. It grew from the "Ralph Wiggum loop" [V]. "The verifier is the bottleneck" [S] | Write goals, checkers and stop conditions. **QA/testers can pivot to AI evals**, i.e. testing AI systems |
| 3 | **Move closer to the customer and the business** | **FDE:** India postings about **19×** in a year (Indeed) [V]; global >1,000% vs 13% for all tech (Lightcast) [V]. OpenAI lists an Applied AI Engineer role in Delhi [V]. **Chief AI Officer:** 76% of companies have one, up from 26% (IBM 2026) [V]. A Telugu techie, **Hariprasad Rebala**, became CAIO at Sonata Software (Aug 2026) [V] | Pair coding with communication and domain knowledge (banking, healthcare, retail). Seniors: AI product manager or AI governance (IAPP AIGP) |
| 4 | **Build proof of work, not just certificates** | **60%** of US hiring pros say AI makes skills harder to judge, so they want proof (WGU) [S]. Lightcast: AI skills pay **28%** more, **43%** with 2+ AI skills [V]. PwC: 62% premium [S] | Build 3 public projects (a RAG app on your domain, an agent with evals, a "deployment" write-up). Post on GitHub/LinkedIn. Use certifications as keyword filters, not as the main proof |
| 5 | **Go where the hiring is, and protect yourself** | India: **GCCs** and Hyderabad +9% [V]. US: **the weighted lottery favours higher wage levels**, and the grace-period rule is under threat [V/S] | India: target GCCs and AI teams. US students: aim for roles above Level I wage, and consider cap-exempt employers (universities and research). Everyone: keep a 60-day plan (savings, referrals, a transfer-ready resume) |

## 4. Analogy

**Auto-rickshaw drivers when ride-hailing apps came to Hyderabad:** the drivers who learned the app kept getting rides; the ones who waited at the stand waited longer.

| Auto story | IT story |
|---|---|
| App = new way to get rides | AI = new way work gets done |
| Driver who learned the app | Engineer who uses AI daily |
| Waiting at the stand | Only doing repetitive tasks |

Keep it short (about 20 s) and illustrative. It is not a claim about driver earnings.

## 5. Myths vs reality
| Myth | Reality |
|---|---|
| "AI is taking all IT jobs." | Top-5 Indian IT headcount is flat (+0.6%), not collapsing. AI/ML hiring is +20–32%. AI is cutting **tasks**, mostly junior and repetitive ones. |
| "Every layoff is because of AI." | In Oct 2026 AI was cited for only 1,265 US cuts. Cost, slow demand and "AI washing" all play a part. |
| "Learn prompt engineering and you're safe." | The title is fading. Learn context engineering, loop engineering and evals. |
| "A certificate will get me a job." | Recruiters want proof of work. |
