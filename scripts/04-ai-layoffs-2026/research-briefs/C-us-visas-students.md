## 1. H-1B

### 1a. The $100,000 payment

**Origin**
- Proclamation 10973, "Restriction on Entry of Certain Nonimmigrant Workers," was signed 19 Sept 2025 and took effect 21 Sept 2025 for 12 months. [S, my prior knowledge; confirm at whitehouse.gov]

**Who pays and who is exempt (USCIS guidance of 20 Oct 2025)** [S, my prior knowledge; confirm at uscis.gov]
- The **employer** pays, not the worker.
- It applies only to **new** petitions filed on or after 21 Sept 2025 for beneficiaries **outside the US** who don't hold a valid H-1B visa, or for petitions that ask for consular processing.
- It does **not** apply to change of status inside the US. That covers **F-1 to H-1B for students already in the US**, extensions and change of employer.
- National-interest exceptions are possible.
- For the video, this is the most important point for MS students: an F-1 student changing status inside the US was never charged the fee, even before the court rulings.

**Court timeline**
- **D.C. district court** upheld the fee (Chamber of Commerce case). The appeal was argued in the D.C. Circuit in March 2026, and I found no decision.
  - Date [U]: reported as Feb 2026; my memory says Dec 2025.
  - Source: https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/trump-s--100k-h-1b-visa-fee-may-be-here-to-stay
- **8 Jun 2026, D. Mass. (Judge Leo Sorokin)**: ruled the fee unlawful, calling it a "tax on H-1B petitions without the requisite delegation by Congress," and vacated the policy. [V]
  - https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/federal-court-strikes-down-trump-h1b-fee
  - https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/federal-judge-strikes-down-trumps-100000-fee-on-new-h-1b-visas-for-high-skilled-workers
  - https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/federal-court-vacates-100000-h-1b-fee-requirement/
- **Jul 2026, First Circuit**: refused to pause the ruling, so the government cannot require the payment. [V]
  - https://www.jurist.org/news/2026/07/us-appeals-court-continues-ban-on-100k-fee-for-h-1b-visas/
  - https://www.newsweek.com/donald-trump-100000-h-1b-visa-fee-court-blow-12243874
- **18 Sept 2026**: **Proclamation 11069** extended the restriction through **21 Sept 2027**. [V, two or more law-firm sources]
  - https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/federal-court-issues-new-order-blocking-agency-implementation-of-100000-h-1b-fee/
  - https://remunance.com/industry-news/h1b-100000-fee-blocked-extended-2027/
- **30 Sept 2026, N.D. Cal. (Judge Haywood Gilliam Jr.)**: blocked the agencies from enforcing the payment until they follow Administrative Procedure Act rulemaking. [V]
  - https://www.forbes.com/sites/stuartanderson/2026/10/01/judge-strikes-down-latest-version-of-100000-h-1b-immigration-fee/
  - https://gceb1.com/second-federal-judge-strikes-down-the-100000-h-1b-fee-what-the-september-30-ruling-means
  - It is disputed whether the order invalidates Proclamation 11069 itself (Forbes says yes) or only the DHS, USCIS, State and CBP implementing memos (others say the proclamations were not enjoined). [U]
- **9 Oct 2026**: the government appealed to the Ninth Circuit. The fight is now before the First, Ninth and D.C. Circuits. [S]
  - https://immigration-analytics.com/2026/10/12/administration-takes-100000-h-1b-fee-fight-to-the-ninth-circuit-putting-the-payment-before-three-appeals-courts/
- **Refunds**: no mechanism has been set up. [S] https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/federal-court-vacates-100000-h-1b-fee-requirement/
- **Bottom line on 22 Nov 2026:** the payment is not being collected, but it is legally alive. The White House says it expects to win on appeal.

### 1b. The new DHS fee rule ($103,265), PROPOSED

- Federal Register, 25 Aug 2026: "Fee for Certain H-1B Petitions," FR Doc 2026-17324. [V]
  - https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/25/2026-17324/fee-for-certain-h-1b-petitions
- **Amount**: $103,265 per cap-subject petition, including advanced-degree (master's cap) petitions. [V]
  - DHS's math: about $8.78B in annual costs divided by about 85,000 cap petitions. [S]
- **Who pays**: the employer.
- **Who is covered**:
  - Cap-exempt petitions (universities and the like) are not covered. [V]
  - Extensions and change-of-employer petitions are likely not covered, based on one firm's reading. [U]
  - Whether F-1 to H-1B change of status is exempt is **not clear** from the sources I saw. [U]
- It is **in addition to** any proclamation payment. [S]
  - https://www.murthy.com/2026/08/24/newsflash-dhs-proposes-103265-fee-for-certain-h-1b-petitions/
  - https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/dhs-proposes-permanent-100000-fee-for-h1b-cap-subject-petitions/
- **Status**: comments closed around 24 Sept 2026, and there was **no final rule as of about 9 Nov 2026**. [S] https://visaenvoy.com/h-1b-100000-fee-blocked-proposed-103265-fee/
- Why it matters: this rulemaking is the government's attempt to cure the procedural defect the courts found. If it becomes final, the fee could return in a court-proof form. Watch for it.

### 1c. Wage-weighted lottery and FY2027 results (March 2026 registration)

**The rule (IN FORCE)** [S, my prior knowledge; confirm on federalregister.gov]
- The final rule was published in Dec 2025.
- Each beneficiary is entered into the lottery based on the OEWS wage level of the offered job:
  - Level IV: 4 entries
  - Level III: 3 entries
  - Level II: 2 entries
  - Level I: 1 entry
- The FY2027 registration form added wage-level reporting. [V] https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/h-1b-cap-update-selection-notifications-for-fy-2027/

**Timeline**
- Registration opened **4 Mar 2026**. [V] https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCIS/bulletins/406da02

## 2. F-1 / OPT / STEM OPT

### 2a. Rules

**Duration of status (D/S)**
- Final rule published 17 Jul 2026, 91 FR 44976. [V]
  - https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/17/2026-14439/establishing-a-fixed-time-period-of-admission-and-an-extension-of-stay-procedure-for-nonimmigrant
- What it would have done:
  - Fixed admission for the program length, up to a **4-year cap**.
  - Post-study grace period cut from **60 to 30 days**.
  - Any longer stay would need an extension filed with USCIS (EOS).
- **Blocked 14 Sept 2026** by a preliminary injunction (D. Mass., Judge Saylor), one day before it was due to take effect. [V]
  - https://www.aip.org/fyi/court-temporarily-blocks-rule-ending-duration-of-status-for-international-students
  - https://wire.auburn.edu/content/announcements/2026/09/21093802-announcement.php
- Government appealed to the First Circuit on 30 Sept. [S]
- Reported **vacated in full on 16 Nov 2026**. [U, single source] https://www.arcfe.com/post/ds-rule-vacated-f1-j1-students-november-2026
- **Today, D/S and the 60-day F-1 grace period still apply.**

**Proposed OPT fee on schools**
- $70,000 for a school's first OPT recommendation for a student; $30,000 for each later one, including STEM OPT. That is about $100,000 per student who uses OPT plus STEM OPT. [V]
- Published 8 Oct 2026. Comments closed 9 Nov 2026. Not in effect.
- The **school** pays, not the student. In practice, universities may stop recommending OPT or pass the cost on. This is the biggest open threat to the "MS then OPT" path. [inference]

## 4. Layoffs and visa holders

### The 60-day grace period (law in force today)

**Rule** [V, from 8 CFR 214.1(l)(2), 2017 rule]
- After a job ends, H-1B, H-1B1, L-1, O-1, TN, E-1, E-2 and E-3 workers (and their dependents) get up to 60 days, or until the I-94 expires if that is sooner.
- The clock starts the day after the last day paid.
- They **cannot work** during the grace period.

**Options inside the 60 days** [V]
1. A new employer files an H-1B transfer. No new lottery is needed, and the worker can start working once the transfer is filed (H-1B portability).
2. Change status, for example to H-4 through a spouse, F-1 for studies, or B-2 as a short bridge. B-2 and H-4 bridge filings now face more scrutiny.
3. Leave the US.

Sources:
- https://www.business-standard.com/immigration/h-1b-visa-layoffs-the-important-plan-b-every-indian-worker-in-the-us-needs-125112400338_1.html
- https://www.fennemorelaw.com/laid-off-h-1b-workers-new-scrutiny-of-b-2-and-h-4-bridge-filings/

### PROPOSED: eliminate the grace period

- DHS proposed rule, Federal Register 11 Sept 2026 (FR Doc 2026-18631, RIN 1615-AD22). [V]
  - https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-11/html/2026-18631.htm
  - https://www.cato.org/commentary/dhs-proposes-immigration-rule-end-60-day-h-1b-grace-period
  - https://news.bloomberglaw.com/daily-labor-report/h-1b-workers-grace-period-after-layoff-nixed-in-dhs-proposal
- **DHS's own estimate: on average 65,752 covered workers a year lost a job or changed employers in 2021–2025.** [S, from rule text via search snippet]
- The 60-day comment period ended around 10 Nov 2026 (my count). No final rule yet.
- If finalized, a laid-off worker would effectively be out of status right away. This is the single most important "watch this" item for H-1B viewers.

## 6. Hook facts (4–6, ready for script)

1. **The H-1B lottery shrank by 38%.** 3.44 lakh registrations for FY2026 fell to 2.12 lakh for FY2027 (Mar 2026). Fewer people are even trying. [V]
2. **Indian graduate students in the US fell 34% in one year**, from 1.78 lakh to 1.17 lakh (Open Doors, 17 Nov 2026). Meanwhile Indians on OPT rose 27% to 1.82 lakh. More Indians are stuck job-hunting than are studying. [V]
3. **The $100,000 H-1B fee is "dead and alive" at the same time.** Two federal judges blocked it (8 Jun and 30 Sept 2026). The President renewed it to Sept 2027 anyway. Three appeals courts are now deciding. A new $103,265 fee rule is waiting in the wings. [V]
4. **Computer engineering grads have the 2nd-highest unemployment of 73 US majors (7.8%)**, higher than most arts majors. CS is 5th at 7.0% (NY Fed, 2026). [V]
5. **The 60-day lifeline might vanish.** DHS proposed on 11 Sept 2026 to end the 60-day grace period for laid-off H-1B workers. By DHS's own count, about 65,000 such workers a year use it. [V rule / S number]
6. **Lower salary means a lower chance in the lottery now.** Under the new weighted lottery, only about 17.7% of those selected were entry-level (Level I) wage. [S]

Bonus hook: there are 2,117 GCCs in India with 23.6 lakh employees, and they hired about twice as many people as Indian IT services firms in FY26. [V/S]
