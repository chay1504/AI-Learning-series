## 6. "Loop engineering": what it really means in 2026

**Verdict: a real and very new term (June 2026).** It is a **practice or skill**, not yet a job title. **I found no "Loop Engineer" job postings.** The script should present it as "the newest skill buzzword engineers are talking about", not as "a job you can apply for".

| Fact | Source | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| IBM's definition: "the practice of designing agentic workflows, or loops, that iteratively guide AI agents toward completing user-defined goals with minimal human intervention". IBM says it underpins coding agents such as **Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and IBM Bob**. The explainer was published July 2026 | IBM Think, https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/loop-engineering | [V] (primary explainer) |
| **Addy Osmani** (Google) essay "Loop Engineering", **June 7 2026**: loop engineering means "replacing yourself as the person who prompts the agent". You define a goal and the AI iterates until it is complete. The building blocks are worktrees, skills, plugins/connectors, sub-agents (one writes, a different one checks), and a memory file or board | https://addyosmani.com/blog/loop-engineering/ ; O'Reilly Radar, https://oreilly.com/radar/loop-engineering | [V] (author's own page) |
| Framed as the layer after prompt engineering → context engineering → harness engineering → **loop engineering**. Key questions: what cycle keeps the agent working, and when exactly does it stop? "The verifier is the bottleneck, not the model" | dev.to, https://dev.to/aijasonz/loop-engineering-guide-2026-43f ; MachineLearningMastery, https://machinelearningmastery.com/an-introduction-to-loop-engineering/ | [S] |
| Sonar's counter-point: "Loop engineering without verification is just automation". The final stop condition should be a deterministic check (tests, linters) | https://www.sonarsource.com/blog/loop-engineering-without-verification-is-just-automation/ | [S] (vendor) |
| Peter Steinberger pushed the idea on June 7; Andrew Ng wrote about it in his June 2026 letter | dev.to / Medium only | [U] |
| **Its ancestor, the "Ralph Wiggum loop":** Geoffrey Huntley (Australia), about **May 2025**. "Ralph is a Bash loop": `while :; do cat PROMPT.md \| claude-code ; done`. It feeds the same prompt to the coding agent again and again until the job is done. Huntley built a programming language ("CURSED") by looping Claude for 3 months. The Register (Jan 27 2026) reported it cloning commercial software "for $10 an hour". Anthropic shipped an official Ralph Wiggum plugin for Claude Code (Dec 2025) using stop hooks | The Register, https://www.theregister.com/2026/01/27/ralph_wiggum_claude_loops/ ; VentureBeat, https://venturebeat.com/ai/how-ralph-wiggum-went-from-the-simpsons-to-the-biggest-name-in-ai-right-now/ ; DreamHost, https://www.dreamhost.com/blog/ralph-wiggum/ | [V] (Huntley/loop); [S] (plugin date) |

**Simple explanation for viewers:** Prompt engineering is asking well once. Context engineering is giving the AI the right files and information. Loop engineering is building the cycle: the AI tries, a checker (tests or a second AI) says pass or fail, and the AI retries until it passes or a limit is hit, while you sleep. The skill to learn is writing a clear goal, a good automatic checker, and a safe stop condition.

Related older concept: "human-in-the-loop" (a person approves steps). That is NOT the same thing. Loop engineering tries to *minimise* human prompting, while keeping humans at checkpoints.

## 7. AI evals engineer / AI quality

- **What it is:** Designs tests and benchmarks that measure whether an AI system is accurate, safe and reliable before and after launch. It is QA/SDET for LLMs.
- **Evidence:** Titles exist and are named in role-fragmentation pieces ("Evals Engineers", "Harness Engineers"; The New Stack, https://thenewstack.io/ai-engineering-roles-emerging/) [S]. **I found no posting count.** Pymnts lists it among the roles AI created [S].
- **Verdict:** Emerging and credible, **but no numbers**. This is a good pivot path for **Indian QA/testing professionals**, and it ties to "the verifier is the bottleneck" in loop engineering.
- **Skills:** test design, statistics basics, LLM-as-judge, eval frameworks, red-teaming basics, Python.

## 15. Disputed / uncertain: do not overstate these

- **FDE growth multiple** varies by source: +350% (Paraform), +729% (Indeed US, via BI), 15x (LinkUp), more than 1,000% (Lightcast), 19x (Indeed India; TNW also says "19x in Jan 2026"). The title is applied inconsistently. Always name the source.
- **The FDE "$550K" pay** headlines are total comp including equity at frontier labs; that is an [U] outlier. The India Glassdoor average (₹12.88 LPA) rests on only 26 salaries.
- **PwC 62% premium:** the press-release summary says "up from 57%", but PwC's 2025 report headline was 56%. Quote only the 62% figure, attributed to PwC.
- **The LinkedIn India #1 role** is Prompt Engineer according to HR Katha and People Matters, while the city lists show AI Engineer at #1. Check the official LinkedIn page before saying "India's #1 job is prompt engineer".
- **Anthropic % of code:** this is a self-report; the range is 70–90% company-wide, and "100%" applies only to individual engineers. Amodei's March 2025 prediction ("90% of all code in 3–6 months") was **not borne out industry-wide** (ITPro).
- **Stack Overflow 2026 numbers** come via IT Brief coverage; I could not open the original page.
- **Loop engineering:** the definitions are still settling. Steinberger and Andrew Ng's roles are [U]. There is no evidence of "Loop Engineer" job titles.
- **CAIO at Infosys, HCLTech, Tech Mahindra:** not found. TCS and Wipro use "head/CEO of AI unit", not "CAIO".
- **The Anthropic "$100M / 10,000 FDEs" pledge** and AWS's "$1B FDE push" come from secondary sources only [U].
- **"AI PM +161%"** has a weak source [U].
- **Gaps (budget):** posting counts for agent engineers, evals engineers, LLMOps and solutions architects; Udemy 2026 report; CAIO salaries; "model behaviour designer" and "agent ops" titles.
