## 7. Hook facts (on-camera candidates)

1. **"In 2026 so far, US companies have blamed AI for 121,401 layoffs, more than double all of 2025 (54,836)."** Challenger, through Oct 2026 [V]
2. **Block cut nearly half its company (more than 4,000 people) and said "we're not in trouble, our business is strong". The stock jumped 24%.** Feb 2026 [V]
3. **Meta cut 8,000 people, cancelled 6,000 job openings, and moved 7,000 others into AI teams, all in one move.** May 2026 [V]
