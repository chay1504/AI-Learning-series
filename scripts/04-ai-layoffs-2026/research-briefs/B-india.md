Researched 2026-11-22. Focus window: Jun–Nov 2026, with FY26 / 2025 as the baseline.

**Tags:**
- **[V]** means verified: the same number appears in two or more independent outlets or in a company release, or follows by arithmetic from verified numbers.
- **[S]** means a single source, or a figure taken from search snippets only.
- **[U]** means unverified or conflicting. Do not say it on camera without a hedge.

**Method caveat:** WebFetch was blocked in this session (hrkatha.com and angelone.in did not resolve), so every figure comes from web-search result summaries. I did not open any primary PDF factsheet. Before the final script, spot-check the 7 company headcounts against the IR factsheets. The Sep-2025 baselines for Infosys and HCLTech come from their Q2 FY26 releases as recalled; they were not re-fetched.

These five firms employ about 15.5 lakh people. They added fewer than 10,000 people in 12 months while posting rupee revenue growth of roughly 10% or more (TCS +11.2%, HCL +11.5%).

**Quarterly aggregates for the top 5:**
- **Q1 FY27:** net +5,480 [S]. Only TCS and Wipro grew; Infosys, HCLTech and Tech Mahindra fell.
- **Q2 FY27:** net **+13,918** [V].
- **H1 FY27:** **+16,055**, against just **3,097** in H1 FY26 [V, 2 outlets].
- **Freshers:** 60–65% of net new hiring were freshers (UnearthInsight) [S].

## 3. GCCs: the counter-story

**National picture (Nasscom-Zinnov "India GCC Landscape 2026", May 2026)**
- **2,117 GCCs** employing **~2.36 million** people, with **$98.4B** revenue in FY26 [V].
- **100+** new centres were added in FY26 [V].
- Employment was about 1.9M in FY24 [S].
- Sources: [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/enterprise-story/2026/05/gccs-number-touch-2117-in-india-revenue-just-short-of-100-billion), [Zinnov](https://zinnov.com/centers-of-excellence/zinnov-nasscom-india-gcc-landscape-2026-report/).
- Business Today framed it as India's GCC story hitting its 2030 target 4 years early. Source: [BT 7-May-26](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/the-big-boom-how-indias-gcc-story-hits-2030-target-4-years-early-530265-2026-05-07).

**GCCs vs IT services**
- GCCs added about **200,000** people in FY26, against about **110,000** at IT services firms [S].
- **2030 projection [U]:** one outlet says **3.5 million** by 2030, another says "2.5M+". Source: [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/nasscom-zinnov-gccs-in-india-employ-236-million-plan-35-million-by-2030/tldr).

**2026 hiring**
- GCC recruitment is projected at **510,452 jobs in 2026**, 3.4 times the 2021 level [S]. Source: [BT 1-Jul-26](https://www.businesstoday.in/jobs/story/at-5-1-lakh-jobs-indias-global-capability-centre-hiring-boom-scales-new-peak-540328-2026-07-01).
- More than half of GCCs say they will hire more in FY27 [S]. Source: [YourStory Aug-26](https://yourstory.com/enterprise-story/2026/08/more-than-half-gccs-signal-increased-hiring-fy27-report).
- **AI and data skills drive 2 in 3 new GCC jobs**; GCC hiring is up 11% [S]. Source: [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/amp/story/economy%2Fai-data-skills-now-drive-two-in-three-new-gcc-jobs-in-india-as-hiring-rises-11-per-cent).

**AI talent and geography**
- Indian GCCs hold **28%** of the global GCC AI talent pool, yet **none** qualifies as "AI-native" [S]. Source: [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/gcc/worlds-largest-ai-hiring-hub-yet-no-gcc-in-india-is-actually-ai-native).
- Bengaluru has about 30% of GCC hiring; Tier-2 cities are growing fastest, about +23% YoY [S].

**But GCC hiring is slowing.** HAN Digital projects GCC net additions of **30–32k in H1 FY27, against 45k in H1 FY26** [S] (see section 4).

## 6. Hook facts (5–8 "wow" lines)

1. **"15.5 lakh employees, +9,320 in a year."** India's top 5 IT firms grew headcount just **0.6%** from Sep 2025 to Sep 2026 while revenue kept growing (TCS and HCL about +11% in rupees). Revenue is up while hiring is nearly flat, which is the "non-linear" model in action. [V, computed]
2. **TCS planned to cut 12,000 and the headcount fell about 31,000.** It dropped about 30,900 in 6 months (Jul–Dec 2025), against the 12,000 it announced. Even after three quarters of re-hiring, it is still about 14,700 below Sep 2024. [V]

4. **Oracle cut up to 12,000 jobs in India**, reportedly about 40% of its India workforce, and started a second round in Sep 2026. [S/U: say "reports say"]
