## [00:55–01:40] Stream 1 · Pre-booking: money before the game even exists

The first way is pre-booking. A lot of people don't know how this actually works.

On June 25, 2026, Rockstar revealed the price: 79.99 dollars for the Standard edition and 99.99 for Ultimate. In India, ₹5,999 and ₹7,499. And the same day pre-orders opened. That means you pay the money in advance, the game downloads to your console before launch, and at midnight on launch day it simply unlocks.

> On screen: pre-orders opened 25 Jun 2026 · $79.99 / $99.99 · India ₹5,999 / ₹7,499

And how many people pre-booked? Just think once and guess. Take-Two says 32.5 million copies were sold before the game even released. 32.5 million. At the Standard price, that's around 2.6 billion dollars of games sold before launch day.

> On screen: 32.5M copies sold before launch ≈ $2.6B at Standard price

And this is why delays are so scary for them. Every time Rockstar announced a delay, Take-Two's stock fell around 7 to 10% in a single day. Because when a game makes this much money before launch, even one delay shakes the whole company.

## [01:40–02:45] Stream 2 · Game sales and the break-even maths

The second way is the actual game sale. Now here's the twist for everyone who thinks GTA earns by selling CDs: even if you buy the "physical" box of GTA 6, there's no disc inside. Only a download code. Around 97% of Take-Two's revenue is now digital.

> On screen: physical box = download code · ~97% of Take-Two revenue is digital

But does Rockstar get the whole ₹5,999? No. When you buy on PlayStation or Xbox, the platform usually takes around 30%. So in a standard industry model, out of an 80-dollar game, around 24 dollars goes to Sony or Microsoft and around 56 dollars goes to Take-Two. Rockstar's exact deal isn't public, but that's the standard model. And from that 56 dollars they still pay for development, marketing and tax.

Now let's do the maths. If GTA 6 cost 1.5 billion dollars, at around 56 dollars a copy they need around 27 million digital copies just to recover it. Even if it cost 2 billion, they need around 36 million. And they sold 32.5 million before launch. That means if the analysts' estimates are right, GTA 6 may have earned back its budget almost on day one.

> On screen: break-even table, $1B → ~18M copies · $1.5B → ~27M · $2B → ~36M · pre-sold 32.5M

And the Ultimate edition? That's an upsell: 20 dollars more for extra content. Small for one buyer, but multiply it by millions of buyers.

## [02:45–04:05] Stream 3 · Online: the real money

But the game sale isn't the biggest money. The real money is online. And to understand it, look at GTA 5.

In GTA Online you can buy something called Shark Cards with real money. The biggest one costs 99.99 dollars and gives you 8 million GTA dollars. That means you pay real money for fake money, to buy fake cars in a fake city. Then there's GTA+, a monthly subscription at 7.99 dollars.

> On screen: Shark Card $99.99 = GTA$8M · GTA+ $7.99/month

And here's the shocking part. In April 2026 hackers leaked Rockstar's internal data. Rockstar confirmed the breach but not the numbers, so take this as leaked data. According to it, GTA Online was making around 1.32 million dollars every day, around 480 million dollars a year. And only around 4% of the players were paying.

> On screen: leaked data: ~$1.32M/day ≈ $480M/year · only ~4% of players pay

To put it simply, think of your mobile phone. You buy the phone once. But you pay for a recharge every month, for years. And after a few years, the recharge money is more than the phone price. GTA works exactly like that. The ₹5,999 game is the phone. GTA Online and GTA+ are the recharge. GTA isn't selling you a CD. It's selling you a SIM card.

> On screen: phone = the game · recharge = Shark Cards / GTA+ · "GTA isn't selling you a CD, it's selling you a SIM card"

That's why 78% of Take-Two's money as a company in that year came from in-game spending and add-ons, not from selling games. And GTA 6 Online? It hasn't been announced yet. The game launched as single-player, and reports say Online comes next year. When it comes, that's the money that keeps flowing for the next 10 years.

## [04:05–04:45] Stream 4 · The long tail: sell the same game again

The fourth way is selling the same game again and again. GTA 5 came out on PS3 in 2013. Then on PS4 in 2014. Then on PC in 2015. Then on PS5 in 2022. Many people bought the same game three times.

Result? GTA 5 has sold nearly 235 million copies, and even now, 13 years later, it sells around 54,000 copies every day. In the year that ended March 2026, GTA products still made around 825 million dollars for Take-Two. That's around ₹21 crore every day from a 13-year-old game.

> On screen: GTA 5 · PS3 → PS4 → PC → PS5 · ~235M copies · ~54,000/day · ~₹21 cr/day

And GTA 6? It's only on PS5 and Xbox right now. There's no PC version announced yet. Take-Two's CEO himself has said PC can be up to around 40% of sales for big games. So that's a whole audience still waiting, including most of us in India, who play on mobile and PC.

## [04:45–05:25] Stream 5 · Brands and culture, but no ads

Now a lot of people think, "Such a big game, it must earn crores from ads inside the game." But that's not it. There are zero real brands inside GTA 6. Take-Two's CEO said it clearly: "It's a fictional world… all the brands are made up." Instead they make fun of brands: Sprunk is Sprite, eCola is Coca-Cola, Lifeinvader is Facebook.

> On screen: no in-game ads · Sprunk = Sprite · eCola = Coca-Cola · Lifeinvader = Facebook

The brand money happens outside the game: an official GTA 6 music album with Atlantic Records, Nike and Travis Scott GTA 6 sneakers at 130 dollars, and official merch. And the biggest free marketing of all? Around 7 million people were watching GTA 6 streams live at the same moment on launch night. Every streamer is basically a free advertisement for Rockstar.

## [05:25–05:50] B15 · So how much will GTA 6 earn?

So how much will it earn in total? For the full year ending March 2027, Take-Two expects around 8 to 8.2 billion dollars in bookings for the whole company, around ₹75,000 crore, and GTA 6 is the biggest part of that. Some analysts say GTA 6 can cross 10 billion dollars in its lifetime. That's around ₹94,000 crore.

But the first real revenue number from Take-Two comes in February 2027. That's the date to watch.

> On screen: FY27 guidance $8.0–8.2B (≈ ₹75,000 cr) · analysts: $10B+ lifetime (≈ ₹94,000 cr) · watch: Feb 2027 results
