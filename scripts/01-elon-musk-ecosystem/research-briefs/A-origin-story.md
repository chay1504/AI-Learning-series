## 2. Bio quick facts

- **Born:** 28 June 1971, Pretoria, South Africa [V] (Britannica). He is **55** as of Nov 2026.
- **To Canada:** June 1989, aged 17, on a Canadian passport through his Canadian-born mother Maye. He went to Queen's University in Kingston, Ontario [S] (Wikipedia mirror; Queen's alumni review).
- **UPenn:** two bachelor's degrees, physics (College of Arts & Sciences) and economics (Wharton) [V]. The graduation year is given as both 1995 and 1997 [U]. Penn formally conferred the degrees in 1997.
- **Stanford:** enrolled in a PhD programme in 1995 and left after **2 days** [V] (Fortune, Feb 2022). Sources disagree on the field: physics, applied physics, or materials science. Safest wording on screen: "Stanford PhD".
- **Childhood hook:** wrote *Blastar* at about 12 and sold it for **$500** (published 1984) [V].
- **US citizenship:** became a US citizen in 2002 [U, not checked this session; from prior knowledge].

## 3. Zip2 (1995–1999)

- An online city guide and business directory with maps, sold to newspapers. It was founded in Palo Alto in 1995 as Global Link Information Network by Elon, Kimbal and Greg Kouri [V] (Britannica; Stanford eCorner transcript).
- **Hustle story:** Elon's own account (Stanford eCorner transcript) is that he couldn't afford both an apartment and an office, so he rented the office, slept on a futon there and showered at the YMCA on Page Mill Road [V]. Jimmy Soni's book *The Founders* describes the same thing. Their sister Tosca recalled "two mattresses and lots of Cocoa Puffs boxes" [S] (https://ca.news.yahoo.com/elon-musks-office-sleeping-habits-100800223.html).
- **Seed money:** Vance's biography says their father Errol gave **$28,000**. Musk disputes this and says Errol put in about $20k later, in a $200k angel round [U].
- **Exit:** Compaq bought Zip2 in Feb 1999 for **$307M** cash (Britannica; Wikipedia's text says $305M). Elon got **~$22M**, reported as a ~7% stake; Kimbal got ~$15M [V].

## 4. X.com, then PayPal (1999–2002)

- **X.com** was founded in 1999 with about **$10M** of his Zip2 money [S]. The exact month (often given as March 1999) was not checked [U].
- **Merger:** X.com merged with **Confinity** (Peter Thiel, Max Levchin, Luke Nosek and others), which owned the PayPal product, in **March 2000** [V].
- **Ouster:** In **September 2000**, while Musk was flying to a delayed honeymoon (his wedding to Justine was in January 2000), the board replaced him with Thiel. Employees loyal to Thiel had threatened to quit. Musk flew back but lost the vote [V] (Fox Business, citing Ashlee Vance's 2015 book; Snopes). Snopes notes the company was still called X.com at the time and became "PayPal" in 2001.
- **Sale:** PayPal IPO'd in Feb 2002. On **8 July 2002**, eBay announced it would buy PayPal for **$1.5B** in stock (AP at the time: "$1.3B stock deal" that valued PayPal at $1.5B); the deal closed in **Oct 2002** [V].
- **Musk's take:** **~$180M** is the most-cited figure (AOL and Toptal cite ~$180M; an often-quoted ~11.7% stake; one note says $250M pre-tax and $180M after tax). The precise "$175.8M" figure could not be confirmed this session [S/U]. Safest on-screen wording: "around $180 million".
- **"PayPal Mafia":** the term comes from a 2007 *Fortune* cover story by Jeffrey M. O'Brien. Its photo of 13 members at Tosca bar in San Francisco was taken in Oct 2007; Musk was **not** in it [V] (https://fortune.com/2024/07/21/paypal-mafia-silicon-valley-thiel-hoffman-botha-rabois-musk/). Members include:
  - Peter Thiel: "the Don", Palantir, Founders Fund, first outside investor in Facebook
  - Reid Hoffman: LinkedIn
  - Chad Hurley, Steve Chen, Jawed Karim: YouTube
  - Jeremy Stoppelman, Russel Simmons: Yelp
  - Max Levchin: Affirm
  - David Sacks: Yammer
  - Keith Rabois, Roelof Botha (Sequoia)
  - Musk himself (Tesla, SpaceX)

  Companies are from the Fortune and Wikipedia lists; Palantir, Affirm and Yammer come from prior knowledge, which is widely documented but was not re-checked this session.
- **Buying back the x.com domain:** On **10–11 July 2017**, Musk tweeted that he had bought x.com back from PayPal: "no immediate plans, but it has great sentimental value to me". The price was not disclosed [V] (TechCrunch, Inc). The domain later became the brand of Twitter after the July 2023 rename. **Story beat:** a domain from his 1999 startup became the name of his social network 24 years later.

## 5. Reinvesting the PayPal money (2002–2008)

The commonly cited split of the ~$180M is about **$100M to SpaceX, $70M to Tesla and $10M to SolarCity** [S] (AOL; Toptal). The $70M is Tesla money across several rounds, not just the Series A.

### SpaceX
- Founded in 2002 in a 75,000 sq ft warehouse in El Segundo. The founding date is either 14 Mar 2002 (Wikipedia infobox) or 6 May 2002 (incorporation, per older versions) [U on the exact day]. Musk had put in about **$100M** of his own money by 2006 [V].
- **The three failures:** Falcon 1 failed in 2006, 2007 and Aug 2008. The **4th launch, on 28 Sep 2008**, reached orbit: the first privately developed liquid-fuelled rocket to orbit Earth. The payload was a 364-lb dummy called "RatSat" [V] (Space.com, Spaceflight Now, TechCrunch 2008). Musk has said he only had money for about 3 attempts. That quote is widely repeated but was not re-checked here [U].
- **NASA lifeline, 23 Dec 2008:** a Commercial Resupply Services contract for **12 flights worth ~$1.6B**. NASA's Gerstenmaier: "We're looking at potentially 12 flights from SpaceX valued at $1.6 billion." The contract ceiling was ~$3.1B [V].

### Tesla
- Incorporated **1 Jul 2003** by **Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning** [V].
- In the **Feb 2004 Series A**, Musk put in **$6.5M of the $7.5M** raised (Fortune: "almost 90 percent"), becoming **chairman** and largest shareholder [V]. Toptal says $6.35M in Apr 2004 [conflict, minor].
- A 2009 lawsuit settlement allows five people to call themselves co-founders: Eberhard, Tarpenning, Ian Wright, Musk and JB Straubel [V].
- Musk became Tesla CEO in Oct 2008, after Eberhard was pushed out in 2007 [U, prior knowledge, not re-checked].
- **Christmas Eve 2008:** a **~$40M** convertible-debt round closed at **6 pm on 24 Dec 2008**. Musk says payroll would have bounced about 2 days later. He has said he put in "the last of my remaining cash" and didn't own a house [V] (InsideEVs, CleanTechnica, Musk tweets). A careful framing (atticusli.com): Tesla was "hours from losing the round, ~3 days from missing payroll", not literally hours from bankruptcy.

### SolarCity
- Founded in 2006 by his cousins **Lyndon and Peter Rive**. Musk was a seed investor (~$10M) and chairman [S].
- **Tesla bought it on 21 Nov 2016** for **~$2.6B in stock** (0.11 Tesla shares for each SolarCity share), after an 85% yes vote on 17 Nov. Musk owned ~20% of both companies and recused himself [V]. Business Insider valued the deal at about $2B plus ~$3B of debt taken on.
- It later became Tesla Energy. The deal drew a shareholder lawsuit that Musk won in Delaware [U, prior knowledge].

### "2008: the year everything nearly died" (narration sequence)
In one year: the third rocket failure (Aug), a divorce, the recession, Tesla nearly out of cash. Then the 4th rocket reached orbit (28 Sep), NASA's $1.6B contract came on 23 Dec, and Tesla's funding closed on 24 Dec. NASA's contract and Tesla's rescue came on consecutive days. [V for the dated items; the divorce is from prior knowledge (Justine, 2008) and was not re-checked.]

## 6. OpenAI (2015 to the 2026 lawsuit)

- **Founded:** announced **11 Dec 2015** as a nonprofit with **"over $1B pledged"** by Musk, Altman, Brockman, Sutskever and others (Reid Hoffman, Peter Thiel, AWS, Infosys, YC Research). Musk was co-chair [V].
  - **India hook (prior knowledge, verify on the 2015 post):** Infosys was one of the original pledgers named in OpenAI's launch post.
- **How much did Musk actually give?** The figures disagree:
  - OpenAI's blog (5 Mar 2024): "**less than $45M** from Elon and more than $90M from other donors" [V] (https://openai.com/index/openai-elon-musk/ ; TechCrunch 5 Mar 2024).
  - At trial (2026), the figure discussed was about **$38M** in actual donations (Courthouse News: $37M in quarterly cash plus rent) [S] (https://news.bloomberglaw.com/litigation/musk-recounts-losing-trust-in-motives-of-openais-other-leaders ; https://courthousenews.com/final-witnesses-testify-in-musk-altman-feud/).
  - Semafor (2023) said $100M [outlier].
  - **Safe wording:** "pledged up to $1 billion, actually gave roughly $40–45 million".
- **Left the board in Feb 2018**, citing the conflict with Tesla's AI work [V] (OpenAI's timeline).
- **Lawsuit:**
  - Filed in 2024 (first in state court, then federal).
  - Jan 2026 filing sought **$79B–$134B** in damages, to be paid to OpenAI's nonprofit, plus removal of Altman and Brockman and an unwinding of the for-profit conversion [V] (Bloomberg, 17 Jan 2026).
  - Trial began in **late April 2026** in Oakland, under Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers.
  - **On 18 May 2026, the 9-person advisory jury found unanimously, in under 2 hours, that the claims were time-barred.** The judge adopted the verdict and dismissed the breach-of-charitable-trust and unjust-enrichment claims [V] (Al Jazeera, PBS/AP, TechCrunch).
  - Musk called it a "calendar technicality" and vowed a 9th Circuit appeal. His lawyer, Marc Toberoff: "One word: Appeal."
  - Separately, xAI appealed to the 9th Circuit over a dismissed trade-secret case [V] (DatacenterDynamics).
  - **Current status of the appeal (as of Nov 2026): not confirmed** [U].
- Quotable trial lines:
  - Musk on the stand: it's not OK to "loot a charity" (Al Jazeera, 28 Apr 2026).
  - OpenAI's lawyer Bill Savitt called the suit a hypocritical attempt to sabotage a competitor.

## 7. Twitter, then X, then xAI, then SpaceX

- **Twitter:** the deal closed on **27 Oct 2022**, at **$44B** ($54.20 a share), just ahead of a court deadline of 28 Oct. CEO Parag Agrawal was fired [V].
- **Renamed X:** announced **23 Jul 2023** and live 24 Jul. The bird became a white X on black. CEO Linda Yaccarino backed it and talked up an "everything app" covering payments and banking [V].
- **xAI acquires X:** **28 Mar 2025**, all stock. xAI was valued at **$80B** and X at **$33B** ($45B enterprise value minus $12B debt) [V].
  - Talking point: X's valuation was "lower than the $44B paid".
- **SpaceX acquires xAI:** **2 Feb 2026**. The combined company was valued at **$1.25T**: SpaceX $1T plus xAI $250B. Before that, xAI was valued at ~$230B and SpaceX at ~$800B in an insider sale [V] (Axios, Bloomberg Tax, Fortune 2 Feb 2026).
  - **This means X (formerly Twitter) is now inside SpaceX (SPCX).**
- Semafor called the merger's headline figures "essentially made up", since Musk controls both sides [S].

## 8. DOGE (2025)

- Served as a **"special government employee"** from **20 Jan 2025**. That status allows a maximum of 130 days in a 365-day period [V].
- Announced his exit in an X post on **28 May 2025**, thanking Trump for his "scheduled time". **30 May 2025** was the 130-day mark [V] (NPR, ABC7, Bloomberg Law).
- The day before, he had criticised the "Big Beautiful Bill" spending bill [V] (CBS).
- DOGE was scheduled to run until 4 Jul 2026 [S].
- Note: NBC counted "more than 114 days", and estimates of the exact end date ranged from 30 May to 13 Jun [U].

## 9. Tesla 2025 CEO pay package (the "$1 trillion" package)

- **Approved on 6 Nov 2025** with more than 75% of votes cast [V] (TechCrunch, NBC, Axios, CBS).
- **12 tranches** of shares worth up to **~12% of Tesla** (some reports say it could take his stake toward ~25%). Each tranche is tied to market-cap steps up to **$8.5T** plus operational and profit goals [V]:
  - 20M vehicles delivered
  - 10M active FSD subscriptions
  - **1M Optimus robots** delivered
  - **1M robotaxis** in commercial operation
  - 8 adjusted-EBITDA (profit) milestones
- He must stay about **7.5 years** to vest anything [V].
- Reuters' net value estimate is about **$878B** after required payments [V].
- Opposed by Norway's sovereign wealth fund, ISS and Glass Lewis [V].
- Shareholders also approved a replacement for the **2018 package (~$56B)**, which a Delaware court had voided; that case was then before the Delaware Supreme Court [V]. **The Delaware Supreme Court outcome (reportedly Dec 2025) was not confirmed this session** [U].

## 12. Hook facts for the Telugu script

1. **Age 12, $500:** he wrote a video game (*Blastar*) and sold its code to a magazine for $500 (1984) [V].
2. **Stanford PhD, 2 days:** he quit after 48 hours to start an internet company (1995) [V].
3. **Slept in the office:** at Zip2 he slept on a futon in the office and showered at the YMCA, because he couldn't afford both a flat and an office [V, his own account].
4. **$22M at 27:** Compaq paid $307M for Zip2, and Musk got ~$22M in 1999 (he turned 28 that June) [V].
5. **Fired on his honeymoon:** the board ousted him as CEO of X.com/PayPal while he was flying to his honeymoon (Sept 2000) [V].
6. **"$180M, and he bet almost all of it":** roughly $100M into SpaceX, $70M into Tesla and $10M into SolarCity, leaving him short of cash by 2008 [S].
7. **3 failures, then orbit:** three Falcon 1 failures, then success on the 4th try (28 Sep 2008), the first private liquid-fuelled rocket to orbit [V].
8. **Two Christmas miracles:** NASA's $1.6B contract on 23 Dec 2008, then Tesla's rescue funding at 6 pm on 24 Dec 2008, "the last hour of the last day" [V].
9. **x.com came home:** he bought back his 1999 domain in 2017 for "sentimental value", and in 2023 it became the new name of Twitter [V].
10. **First trillionaire:** SpaceX's June 2026 IPO raised $75B, the largest ever, and Forbes declared Musk the world's first trillionaire. SpaceX was worth ~$2.1T at the first-day close [V].
11. **Merger chain:** Twitter ($44B, 2022) became X, X was merged into xAI ($33B, 2025), and xAI was merged into SpaceX ($1.25T, Feb 2026). Now Tesla–SpaceX merger committees exist (Oct 2026) [V]. This is the "ecosystem" thesis in one line.
12. **Pledged $1B to OpenAI, gave less than $45M:** then he sued for up to $134B and lost on a technicality: the jury took under 2 hours (May 2026) [V].
13. **The $1T pay package:** it pays only if Tesla reaches an $8.5T market cap and ships 1M robots and 1M robotaxis [V].
