## 1. Headline structure as of Nov 2026 (most important for the script)

- **xAI no longer exists as an independent company.** SpaceX acquired xAI in an all-stock deal announced on **2 Feb 2026**. xAI became a wholly owned SpaceX subsidiary. **[V]**
  - Valuation: SpaceX about **$1T** plus xAI about **$250B**, for a combined **about $1.25T**. Bloomberg was first to report it, and CNBC called it "the biggest merger of all time." **[V]**
  - Terms: xAI shares were valued at **$526.59** each and converted into SpaceX shares at **0.1433** SpaceX shares per xAI share (CNBC, citing documents). **[V]**
  - CNBC also cited bank valuation ranges: SpaceX $859B to $1.26T and xAI $219B to $294B. **[S]**
  - Rationale: Musk's memo on the SpaceX website said the merger is largely about **space-based data centers**, with "space the only logical solution" for scaling power-hungry AI and a vision of up to about **1 million satellites**. **[V]**
  - Sources: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/03/musk-xai-spacex-biggest-merger-ever.html · https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/02/elon-musk-spacex-acquires-xai-data-centers-space-merger/ · https://fortune.com/2026/02/02/elon-musk-spacex-xai-ipo-trillion · https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/03/muskonomy-shakeup-spacex-valuation-after-xai-merger-nears-tesla.html · https://www.euronews.com/2026/02/04/spacex-hits-106tn-after-xai-merger-as-musk-consolidates-empire
  - Date caveat: most outlets say 2 Feb 2026. One aggregator says the deal concluded on 30 Jan 2026. **[U]**
- **Branding:** Grok and X now sit in a SpaceX AI unit branded **"SpaceXAI"**. VentureBeat's headline reads "SpaceXAI debuts Grok 4.6." Some outlets still write "xAI." **[S]**
  - https://venturebeat.com/technology/spacexai-debuts-grok-4-6-overtaking-kimi-k3s-performance-and-matching-gpt-5-6-sol-for-worlds-third-best-on-artificial-analysis
- **Who owns what now:** SpaceX (public) owns xAI/SpaceXAI, which owns **X** (since March 2025), **Grok** and the **Colossus** data centers. In its S-1, SpaceX reports an **"AI segment"** covering Grok, consumer and enterprise AI, X, and "AI computational infrastructure." **[V]**
  - SEC S-1: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001181412/000162828026036936/spaceexplorationtechnologi.htm
- **SpaceX IPO:** priced at **$135/share on 11 Jun 2026** and began trading on **Nasdaq on 12 Jun 2026 under the ticker SPCX**. It raised about **$75B**, which several outlets call the largest IPO ever (one header says $86B, so treat that figure as unverified). It was valued at about **$1.77T** at pricing. On day one it opened at $150 and closed near **$160.95 (+19%)**, a market cap of **about $2.1T** (CNBC). **[V/S]**
  - https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-stock-jumps-2-trillion.html · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering_of_SpaceX
  - Wikipedia says shares had fallen about 50% from the post-IPO peak by late July 2026. Other trackers do not support this. **[U]**

Figures: $1.91T (StockAnalysis, 13 Nov), $1.90T (TipRanks, 13 Nov), about $1.89T at roughly $137/share (TradingView), $1.95T (Public.com, 18 Nov), $1.735T (CompaniesMarketCap, 20 Nov). **[V for the range]**

## 3. Grok products

- **Model cadence:** Grok-1 (2023), Grok-2 (2024), Grok 3 (Feb 2025), Grok 4 (Jul 2025), 4.1 (17 Nov 2025), 4.20 (Apr 2026, after many slips), 4.5 (Jul 2026, single source), 4.6 (Aug 2026), **4.7 (21 Sep 2026, current flagship)**, 4.8 (beta, unconfirmed). Release dates for Grok 1 to 4 come from prior knowledge and were not re-verified in this session.
- **Grok 5:** Musk publicly spoke of about 6 trillion parameters. The January 2026 Series E update said it was "in training." It is still unreleased as of Nov 2026. The pattern of missed dates is itself a usable point for the script. https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/elon-musk-reveals-grok-5-coming-2026-jaw-dropping-6-trillion-parameters-1755754
- **Grok in Tesla cars:** arrived 12 Jul 2025 (update 2025.26) as a beta. It was conversation only, with no car control at first. Users could pick personalities such as "Storyteller" and "Unhinged." The 2026 Spring Update added a "Hey Grok" wake word. The 2026 Summer Update let Grok handle climate, navigation and most everyday car tasks by voice. Older Intel Atom cars are excluded. https://electrek.co/2026/07/21/tesla-2026-summer-update-release-notes/ · https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4638/tesla-now-uses-grok-think-fast-20-with-summer-update
- **Grok in Optimus:** Musk said in June 2025 that Optimus "already" talks using Grok. A September 2025 demo shown by Marc Benioff had noticeable lag. https://dataconomy.com/2025/09/04/tesla-optimus-robot-integrates-xai-grok-assistant
- **Grokipedia:** launched 27 Oct 2025 as v0.1 with 885,279 articles, against more than 7M on English Wikipedia. It crashed within hours. Some entries were near copies of Wikipedia under a CC licence. Users cannot edit. Musk promised "v1.0 will be 10X better." https://heise.de/-10960884 · https://san.com/cc/grokipedia-launches-with-885k-ai-written-entries-critics-flag-bias/
- **Grok Imagine:** image and video generation. Search budget ran out before I could get its launch date. **[not verified this session]**
- **Macrohard / Digital Optimus:** announced Aug 2025 as a "purely AI software company" meant to simulate firms like Microsoft using swarms of Grok agents. On 11 Mar 2026 Musk recast it as a **joint xAI-Tesla project** called "Digital Optimus," "coming as part of Tesla's investment agreement with xAI." Grok is "System 2" (reasoning) and Digital Optimus is "System 1" (fast screen, keyboard and mouse actions). Musk says it runs on Tesla cars with AI4 hardware. Later reports say xAI's Macrohard effort stalled while Tesla's Digital Optimus team grew. Electrek argues this makes Tesla a *customer* of Musk's private AI company. https://electrek.co/2026/03/11/musk-confirms-xai-tesla-joint-digital-optimus-project-shareholder-lawsuit/ · https://www.aol.com/news/xais-macrohard-project-stalls-tesla-100301655.html
  - "MACROHARD" painted on the Colossus 2 roof: I could not verify this in this session. **[U]**
