## 2. Founding and early history

- **Founders:** Incorporated as Tesla Motors on **July 1, 2003** in San Carlos, CA by **Martin Eberhard** (CEO) and **Marc Tarpenning** (CFO). Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Tesla,_Inc. and https://www.britannica.com/topic/Tesla-Motors
- **Musk joins (Feb 2004):** He supplied about 90% of a roughly **$7.5M Series A** and became chairman and largest shareholder. Most accounts put his share at about $6.5M (Fortune, Wikiwand); others say $6.3M or $6.4M. Source: https://fortune.com/2024/07/19/tesla-elon-musk-electric-vehicles-automotive-evs/
- **"Founder" title:** A 2009 legal settlement allows five people to call themselves co-founders: Eberhard, Tarpenning, Ian Wright, JB Straubel and Musk. Source: same Fortune link.
- **Original Roadster (2008–2012):** Built on a Lotus Elise-based chassis under a July 2005 deal with Lotus, though only about 6% of parts were shared. **About 2,450 were built.** Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla_Roadster_(first_generation)
- **Model S (2012) and Model X (2015):** Both were discontinued in 2026. Jalopnik describes it as the end of "a 14-year run for the S and an 11-year run for the X." Musk announced the end in January 2026 and handed the Fremont space to Optimus, with about 600 cars left in inventory at the end. Sources: https://www.jalopnik.com/2173749/last-tesla-model-s-and-model-x-off-production-line/ and https://www.motor1.com/news/785771/tesla-model-s-and-model-x-dead/ (the exact final-build date was not captured).
- **Model 3 (2017) "production hell":** This is standard history and was not separately re-sourced in this session.
- **Model Y:** The world's best-selling vehicle of 2023 at about 1.22M units (JATO), the first EV ever to hold that title. It beat the Toyota RAV4 and Corolla. Source: https://www.jato.com/tesla-model-y-to-be-crowned-worlds-best-selling-vehicle-of-2023/
- **Cybertruck (deliveries from late 2023):** On **2026-11-06**, Musk said production will **wind down around mid-2027** to free capacity for Cybercab. Sales figures:
  - US sales of about 20,237 in 2025, down 48% (KBB: https://www.kbb.com/car-news/tesla-cybertruck-to-be-canceled-in-2027/).
  - Cox Automotive estimates 11,586 in Jan–Sep 2026.
  - The annual run-rate is about 30k against a line built for 250k a year (Electrek: https://electrek.co/2026/11/06/tesla-ending-cybertruck-production-sales-10-percent-plan/).
  - SpaceX bought 1,279 Cybertrucks in Q4 2025 (Electrek, same link).

**Why sales fell in 2024–2025** (from AP/PBS, TechCrunch and CBS coverage):
1. The US $7,500 federal EV tax credit ended on 2025-09-30. Buyers pulled purchases into Q3 2025, and Q4 collapsed afterwards.
2. Chinese competitors grew, BYD above all. BYD sold about **2.26M EVs in 2025** and took the "world's largest EV maker" title (https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/tesla-loses-title-as-worlds-biggest-electric-vehicle-maker-after-sales-fall-for-second-year-in-a-row). In 2024, counting plug-in hybrids, BYD sold 4.25M EVs and PHEVs against Tesla's 1.79M (NBC link above).
3. Some consumers turned against the brand over Musk's political role in the Trump administration (DOGE).
4. The product line aged, and the refreshed Model Y changeover hurt early 2025.
- **Counterpoint for 2026:** Fool.com reports Tesla's share of the US EV market at **59%, its highest** (2026-08-21): https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/21/tesla-controls-59-of-the-us-ev-market-its-highest/. European sales were described as "booming" in Q3 2026 (autoevolution).

## 4. New Roadster

- **Announced:** November 2017, at a **$200,000** base price. The original specs promised 0–60 in 1.9 s; that figure was not re-verified this session.
- **Delays:** At the Nov 2025 shareholder meeting, Musk promised a demo on **April 1, 2026**. It then slipped to late May/June, then August, then Oct 1 (Reuters-sourced report, 2026-09-13), and finally **Oct 15, 2026** after "high winds." Sources: https://electrek.co/2026/03/17/tesla-roadster-unveil-next-month-elon-musk-claims/ ; https://arynews.tv/tesla-to-unveil-long-delayed-roadster-on-october-1-musk-says ; https://www.carscoops.com/2026/06/tesla-roadster-delay/
- **Demo on 2026-10-15** at SpaceX's rocket test site in McGregor, Texas:
  - The car drove up a curved ramp until it was **upside down**, launched about **21 ft** into the air, rotated and landed on its wheels.
  - It ran with no human at the wheel, under automated control.
  - Musk said some versions do **0–60 mph in under 1 second** with a **top speed above 230 mph**.
  - **Production is targeted for 2028.** No price was announced.
  - Sources: https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/teslas-new-roadster-makes-flying-debut-hitting-60-mph-under-second ; https://www.statesman.com/business/article/tesla-roadster-event-live-texas-mcgregor-22433111.php ; https://insideevs.com/news/812151/2027-tesla-roadster-price-specs-release-date/
- **Claimed trims and specs** (mostly from blogs and fan sites, so lower confidence):
  - Performance: under 1.6 s.
  - "Thrust": under 0.9 s, with rear cold-gas thrusters.
  - "Rocket": under 0.59 s, invite-only, not road-legal.
  - The 0.81 s run was timed on the event's own clock.
  - Hardware is described as about 600 L of compressed air feeding 10 nozzles, plus "suction fans."
  - Reservations are reported at $50k and $250k but are unconfirmed.
  - Sources: https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4837/tesla-roadster-event-recap-it-flies-flips-and-hits-0-60-in-081s ; https://www.smobserved.com/story/2026/10/19/news/tesla-unveils-next-generation-roadster-with-spacex-thrusters-and-suction-fans-at-mcgregor-demo-production-targeted-for-2028/10175.html
- **Ecosystem angle:** The cold-gas thrusters are a "SpaceX package" built with SpaceX, shown at a SpaceX facility. This is a Tesla × SpaceX crossover.

- **Active FSD customers in Q3 2026: 1.74M**, up from 1.48M in Q2 and 1.04M a year earlier. In North America, more than 75% of new deliveries came with FSD. The one-time purchase option was dropped in Feb 2026. Sources: https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4862/tesla-fsd-hits-record-subs-purchased-with-75-of-new-orders ; https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/21/tesla-q3-2026-earnings/
- Tesla says FSD sees about 9x fewer major collisions than the US average (6.0M miles per major collision against 660k). This is Tesla's own data and has not been independently verified.
- Musk is hoping for EU-wide FSD approval by Q1 (2027): https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-fsd-attach-rate-75-percent-eu-approval/
