## 2. Origins: why SpaceX exists

- **Mars Oasis and the Russia trip:** **[BACKGROUND – not re-verified this session]** The standard story, from Vance's biography and Jim Cantrell's interviews: in 2001 Musk wanted to land a small greenhouse on Mars ("Mars Oasis") to revive public interest in space. He travelled to Moscow (2001–02) to buy refurbished Russian ICBMs (Dnepr). Their prices seemed absurd, so on the flight home he worked out that a rocket's raw materials cost only about 2–3% of its sale price. That led to SpaceX. Use this as narrative and attribute it ("according to Musk's biographer Ashlee Vance").
- **Founded in 2002** to offer low-cost launches, starting with Falcon 1. [HIGH] Source: Falcon 1 article (Wikipedia) https://www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/Falcon_1
- **Falcon 1:** the first three launches (2006, 2007, Aug 2008) failed. The **fourth, on 28 Sep 2008, reached orbit** from Omelek Island in Kwajalein Atoll, carrying a mass simulator. It was the first privately developed liquid-fuelled rocket to reach orbit. Musk said: "The team is elated and ecstatic." [HIGH] Source: Space.com https://www.space.com/5905-spacex-successfully-launches-falcon-1-rocket-orbit.html and NPR https://www.npr.org/2008/10/03/95345334/spacex-celebrates-first-successful-rocket-launch
- Storytelling point (background, widely reported): the company was close to running out of money after three failures, which is why flight 4 was effectively make-or-break.
