# PART 1: NEURALINK

## 1.1 What it is, in simple terms

- **Company:** Neuralink is a brain-computer interface (BCI) company co-founded by Elon Musk in **2016**. Its device reads electrical signals from neurons and turns them into commands, such as moving a cursor, typing or controlling a robotic arm.
- **The implant (N1 "Link"):** A coin-sized sealed device placed in a hole cut in the skull. It sits flush under the scalp, so it cannot be seen from outside.
  - It has **1,024 electrodes spread across 64 threads**, with 16 electrodes per thread. Each thread is "thinner than a human hair"; VentureBeat put them at about 4–6 µm, roughly a quarter of a hair's width. Sources: neuralink.com (P), https://www.neuralink.com/ ; VentureBeat (M), https://venturebeat.com/ai/neuralinks-technology-embeds-tiny-wires-in-the-brain-to-read-electrical-pulses/ (as of 2024–2026).
  - The battery charges **wirelessly through the skin** with an inductive charger, and the device has no external wires. Source: neuralink.com (P).
- **R1 surgical robot:** The threads are too fine to insert by hand, so a robot sews them into the motor cortex while avoiding blood vessels. Neuralink says: "The threads of our implant are so fine that they can't be inserted by the human hand." Source: neuralink.com (P).
- **N1 User App:** Decodes the "intended movement" signals into cursor movement and clicks. The N1, the app and the R1 are investigational and available **only through Neuralink's trials**; nothing is FDA-approved for sale. Source: neuralink.com (P).
- **Analogy for viewers:** "A Bluetooth mouse that you move with your thoughts instead of your hand."

## 1.3 The product tracks (how Neuralink presents itself, 2025–2026)

Neuralink publicly names **two products: Telepathy and Blindsight**, as described in the June 2025 update and Musk's Oct 2026 post calling Blindsight "our second product". Its **clinical trial list** in 2026 has four studies. For the video, the "three things Neuralink does" can fairly be put as **Move (Telepathy + CONVOY), Speak (VOICE) and See (Blindsight)**. I found **no** evidence of a third product called "Deep".

## 1.8 Controversies (brief)

- **FDA rejection, early 2022.** Dozens of issues were raised, covering the battery, wire migration and safe removal. Neuralink later fixed these and got approval in 2023. Sources: Engadget/Reuters, https://www.engadget.com/fda-reportedly-denied-neuralinks-request-to-begin-human-trials-of-its-brain-implant-204454485.html ; The Register, https://www.theregister.com/2023/03/02/neuralink_fda_rejection/
- **Animal testing.** About 1,500 animal deaths over 4 years (Reuters, Dec 2022), including pigs, sheep and monkeys. Reuters also reported staff complaints about rushed experiments. A USDA IG probe was reported. PCRM complaints about monkeys; Neuralink denies abuse. https://www.popsci.com/technology/neuralink-fda-delay/
- **Thread retraction** in patient #1 (May 2024), described above.
- **Transparency.** Most results come from company videos and X posts, with little peer-reviewed data. Blindsight has had "no public trial record" while rivals publish data. https://runtimewire.com/article/musk-neuralink-blindsight-ready-restore-sight-regulators
- **Timelines keep slipping.** Musk predicted 20–30 implants in 2025; about 12–21 happened. Blindsight was due in 2025, then 2026, and has not happened.
- **Competition** (a useful frame): Synchron (stent-based implant), Paradromics (FDA trial clearance Nov 2025, https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-20/neuralink-rival-paradromics-cleared-to-start-brain-implant-trial ), Sam Altman's **Merge Labs** (OpenAI invested, Jan 2026, https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/15/openai-invests-in-sam-altmans-brain-computer-interface-startup-merge-labs/ ), and **China** approved what MIT Tech Review called the world's first invasive BCI chip (June 2026, https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/01/1138133/china-world-first-brain-chip/ ). There is also a CNBC piece (July 2026) on China's BrainCo betting on wearable BCIs instead of implants: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/11/chinas-brainco-bets-on-wearable-brain-tech.html

## 2.2 The Hyperloop story (short)

- **2013:** Musk publishes the "Hyperloop Alpha" white paper: pods in a low-pressure tube at about 700 mph, published open-source. **[NOT RE-VERIFIED]**
- **2015–2019:** SpaceX ran Hyperloop pod student competitions near Hawthorne. **[NOT RE-VERIFIED]**
- TBC said the Hawthorne tunnel was for testing "hyperloop and Loop" transport. In practice TBC moved to **"Loop"**: ordinary Teslas driving in narrow tunnels. Hyperloop One (Virgin) shut down in 2023. The Hawthorne tunnel was reportedly turned into SpaceX employee parking in 2022 (Bloomberg). **[NOT RE-VERIFIED]**
- **Framing for the video:** the dream was "700 mph vacuum pods"; the reality so far is "Teslas in a tunnel at about 35–40 mph" (Loop speed **[NOT RE-VERIFIED]**).

## 2.5 Technology and cost claims

- **Prufrock** tunnel boring machine. TBC says it is designed to tunnel at **more than 1 mile per week**, which would be **6x faster than the previous Godot+**. It "porpoises": it launches from the surface and resurfaces, with no deep launch pit needed. It "fits nicely in the Starship payload bay" (TBC). https://boringcompany.com/prufrock . An outside analysis found real-world rates of about 46 m/day, well below the target. https://research.contrary.com/report/the-boring-company
- **Cost-per-mile claim.** TBC's goal is about **$10M per mile**, against a common benchmark of about **$1B per mile** for conventional urban subway (the range is $600M–$4B). The Nashville target is about **$25M per mile**. Sources: a16z, https://a16z.com/the-boring-company-the-future-underground/ ; Contrary, link above.
  - **Caveat for honesty:** the comparison is not like-for-like. Loop tunnels are about **12 ft** in diameter versus about **23 ft** for subway tunnels, carry cars rather than trains, and have far lower capacity. Stations and safety systems roughly double the cost (about $20M per mile).

## 2.8 Controversies

- **Chemical burns (2023).** Nevada OSHA cited TBC for **8 "serious" violations**, fined **more than $112,000**, after a tip that **15–20 workers** were burned by accelerant chemicals in the tunnels with no protective equipment. TBC contested all eight. Fortune, https://fortune.com/2024/02/27/flirted-death-elon-musk-boring-company-employees-injuries-osha-citations/
- **Fines rescinded (2025).** On May 28, 2025, Nevada OSHA served notice of **more than $400,000** in fines, among its largest in a decade. Hours later TBC's president called the governor's office, and the citations were rescinded (Fortune, Nov 12, 2025). https://fortune.com/2025/11/12/elon-musk-boring-company-tunnels-injuries-osha-citations-fines-rescinded-nevada-governor-office-documents-altered
- **Firefighter injuries.** Training drills on Dec 9–10, 2024 led to a citation. A Nevada DIR legal memo dated Nov 17, 2025 covers it. https://www.business.nv.gov/siteassets/content/files/dir-legal-memo---tbc-the-boring-company-11-17-25.pdf
- **Environment.** ProPublica (Oct 2025) reported regulators accused TBC of **nearly 800 environmental violations** in two years. The penalty was cut to **$242,800** from a potential **$3M or more**. The governor's office disputed the report. https://www.propublica.org/article/elon-musk-boring-company-violations-fines-vegas-loop
- **Oversight.** ProPublica and City Cast (Jan 2025) reported TBC **twice dug on county property without permits** and lobbied out of a county transit permit. Clark County defends its oversight. https://www.propublica.org/article/elon-musk-boring-company-las-vegas-loop-oversight
- **Other scrutiny.** National COSH named TBC in its **"Dirty Dozen"** unsafe employers list. A Nevada legislative committee reviewed 17 complaints and 8 inspections; TBC skipped the hearing. https://news.yahoo.com/musk-boring-company-makes-list-224459885.html ; https://thenevadaindependent.com/es/article/blatant-special-treatment-nevada-lawmakers-raise-safety-issues-with-boring-co-s-vegas-loop
- **Critics' core argument:** low capacity compared with trains, the cars need drivers, it is "just a car tunnel", and the cost comparisons are not like-for-like.

# PART 6: DISPUTED / UNCERTAIN (do not state as fact without a hedge)

1. **Neuralink patient count after Jan 2026.** The only official figure is **21** (Jan 28, 2026). Fortune's 26 (Nov 21, 2026) came from a search snippet I could not open. Musk's "more than 30" is his estimate. One blog's "10 patients, Oct 2026" contradicts everything else; ignore it.
2. **Neuralink valuation in 2026.** No official priced round since the $9B Series E (June 2025). A May 2026 round with Oman Investment Authority is listed by Tracxn with undisclosed terms. Secondary-market figures of $29–56B and claimed Series F/G/H rounds at $35B are **unreliable**. Some trackers wrongly date the Series E to April 2026.
3. **Blindsight.** It has **not** been implanted in a human as of Nov 2026. "Engineering-ready" is Musk's claim, and no trial is registered. Do **not** say "FDA approved"; it has only a Breakthrough *designation*.
4. **The next-gen implant (Oct 2026)**, said to have 3x the channels, with "FDA cleared trial to 25 patients". This rests on **one** blog (allthings-elon) and is unverified. The "3,000 channels in Terry's implant" claim is also S-tier only.
5. **"High-volume production" and automated surgery in 2026.** This is Musk's Dec 31, 2025 promise. A Samsung-made 4th-gen chip is reportedly targeted for late 2027, which conflicts with it.
6. **Speech-trial details** (VOICE, Terry) come from company videos and blogs; there is no peer-reviewed data yet.
7. **A third Neuralink product called "Deep"** has no evidence. Officially there are 2 products (Telepathy, Blindsight) plus trials (CONVOY, VOICE).
8. **Vegas Loop station count.** 11, 13 and 14 all appear depending on the date and source. TBC says 14 (Nov 2026). The approved total is 123 (newer) or 104 (older).
9. **LVCC Loop cost.** $52.5M (LVCVA), $47M (TBC) and $53M (Wikipedia) all appear.
