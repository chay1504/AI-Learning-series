## 4. Brands: parody versus real

### 4a. Why GTA uses fake brands
- **Official position (Strauss Zelnick, Take-Two CEO, iicon conference, Las Vegas, around 1 May 2026):** GTA VI will have **no product placement and no real-brand deals**. "It's a fictional world and everything in it is fictional… all the brands are made up." He said this "keeps us pure", contrasted it with NBA 2K (which does use real brands), and said "Everyone knows if you push too hard with partnerships, it's a disaster." [V] https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-isnt-even-at-risk-of-having-product-placement-because-all-the-brands-are-made-up-take-two-ceo-says-i-think-that-keeps-us-pure/ ; https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/grand-theft-auto-6-brands-product-placement-parody-3943526
- Outlets disagree on who conducted the interview (Variety or IGN), and the exact wording varies between them. Use the GamesRadar wording.
- **Other reasons.** These are analysis, not Rockstar statements [S/U]:
  - Satire needs freedom. Mocking real companies (fast food, social media, phones) is the joke, and a real brand would never pay to be mocked.
  - It avoids legal and licensing risk around violence. A real brand next to crime, drugs and police shootings is a brand-safety problem.
  - Rockstar keeps full creative control and doesn't need ad money. GTA's revenue comes from sales and Shark Cards, not in-game ads.
  - Source: https://svg.com/867642/the-real-reason-gta-makes-up-its-own-products

## 6. Hook facts (with labels)

1. **One game-maker, two numbers:** Take-Two booked **$6.72B** in FY2026 but still posted a **net loss of $298M**. The year before, the loss was **$4.48B**, mostly from writing down the value of the Zynga deal. [V]
2. **78% of Take-Two's money doesn't come from selling games.** It comes from in-game purchases, virtual currency, add-ons and ads ("recurrent consumer spending"). [V]
3. **97% of Take-Two's revenue is digital.** Even the "physical" GTA VI is basically a code in a box. [S]
4. **GTA V, a 2013 game, has sold 230M+ copies**, and was still among Take-Two's biggest earners in 2026. [V]
5. **Leaked Rockstar data says GTA Online was making about $1.3M a day** in 2025–26, and **only about 4% of players** were paying. [U, leak]
6. **A $99.99 Shark Card buys GTA$8 million**, which is fake money in a fake bank. [V]
7. **29% of all US console/PC gamers said they would buy GTA VI**, the highest purchase intent Circana has ever recorded. [V]
8. **One in three British adults has played a GTA game, and 63% of them first played as minors** (YouGov, n=8,548, Nov 2026). [V]
9. **Zero real brands inside GTA VI.** Take-Two's CEO says fake brands "keep us pure". Outside the game, Rockstar partners with Nike/Travis Scott, Atlantic Records and the Miami Heat. [V/S]
10. **Rockstar paid Miami-Dade ~$975,000 for a "Welcome to Vice City" sign on an NBA arena roof**, just under the $1M threshold that would have needed a county vote. [S]
11. **Rockstar bought (or acqui-hired) the FiveM modders it once banned.** FiveM now peaks above 220k concurrent players on Steam. [V/S]
12. **Estimated ~$1.84B and ~19M copies by launch day** (Sensor Tower), more than double GTA V's launch record. [U, estimate]

## 7. Disputed / uncertain

- **GTA VI launch sales:** as of 22 Nov 2026, Take-Two had **not published official launch numbers** in the sources I found. Sensor Tower ($1.84B / 19M), the "$1.39B pre-orders" figure and Newzoo's 51M week-one projection are all estimates. Watch for an official Take-Two/Rockstar statement before scripting.
- **The $5.08B GTA Online figure:** this is leak-based. Its scope (all GTA Online bookings 2014–2026, or Shark Cards only 2014–2024) is disputed between outlets. Rockstar called the data "non-material" and has not confirmed it.
- **GTA+ subscriber count (1.3M):** a single, leak-derived source.
- **FY2025 RCS share:** excerpts give 77%, 79% or 80% depending on whether the base is bookings or revenue. The FY2026 figure (78% of bookings) is solid.
- **Platform split:** the 10-K gives console 39% of revenue; one article wrongly says console 11%. Mobile is about 49% of bookings.
- **Q2 FY27 EPS and Q3 guidance ranges:** these differ across outlets.
- **Miami sign cost:** $975k (county) versus ~$3M (wider deal, TweakTown).
- **Sony marketing deal:** widely reported but not officially confirmed. Terms such as bundles and commission are not public.
- **Demographics:** no official GTA gender or age data exists. Estimates for the female share range from 15–30%. Bain's "4 in 10 of launch-intenders are women" comes from a small sample (n=800).
- **"Where the $80 goes":** this is an industry model (~30% platform fee), not Rockstar's actual contract terms.
- **Piracy:** no credible data found.
- **Kith × Rockstar collab:** unverified. Do not use.
- **The Zelnick "keeps us pure" interview:** the venue (iicon) is consistent across outlets, but the interviewer is reported as either Variety or IGN.
