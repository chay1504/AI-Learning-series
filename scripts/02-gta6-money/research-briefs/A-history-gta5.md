# Brief A: GTA history and GTA V numbers
For: "The money behind GTA 6" (Telugu explainer). Research date: 2026-11-22.

How to read the confidence tags:
- **[V]** verified: two or more independent sources agree
- **[S]** single source, or a company/claimant figure repeated by outlets
- **[U]** unverified, rumour or leak

Two caveats apply throughout:
- **"Sold-in"** (Take-Two's unit metric) counts units shipped to retailers plus digital sales. It is not a count of players.
- **Net revenue** is what Take-Two books after store/platform cuts. It is lower than the retail value consumers paid.

## 2. Origins: the short story
- **DMA Design** was a small Dundee studio founded by **David Jones** that made its name with *Lemmings* (1991). [V]
- GTA started as **"Race'n'Chase"**, a cops-vs-robbers car game. Playtesters found playing the criminal more fun, and the game pivoted to that. [V] on the pivot, per GamesRadar "making of".
- **The publicity trick.** BMG/DMA hired tabloid PR man **Max Clifford** to stir up outrage. Questions were raised in Parliament and the House of Lords. In a 2012 interview, David Jones said of Clifford: "every word he said came true." [V]
  - Clifford was later convicted in 2014 of indecent assaults. Handle that carefully in the script, or omit it.
- **Ownership chain:** BMG Interactive → Take-Two (Mar 1998, about $14.2M in stock). Rockstar Games was formed Dec 1998. DMA went Gremlin (1997) → Infogrames (1999) → Take-Two (Sep 1999, about $11.9M of debt assumed). DMA became **Rockstar North** in 2002. [V]
- **Script angle:** the whole franchise behind a roughly $10B+ revenue stream was bought for about **$26M** in stock and debt ($14.2M + about $11.9M). That sum is my own arithmetic from two sourced deals, and it ignores Take-Two's later investment, so frame it carefully.

## 3. Turning points (II to IV)
- **GTA III (22 Oct 2001, PS2)** was the 3D open-world breakthrough. It was delayed about 3 weeks after 9/11 for edits, including the cover art. It sold about 15M lifetime (TechRadar) [S].
- **Vice City (2002):** 17.5M+ by Mar 2008 per Take-Two; 13M on PS2 per Guinness. [V]
- **San Andreas (2004):** 17.33M on PS2, the best-selling PS2 game ever (Guinness 2009). About 27.5M across all platforms. [V]
- **Hot Coffee (2005):**
  - The ESRB rated the game AO and US retailers pulled it. [V]
  - Take-Two booked **$24.5M** in costs from returns. [V]
  - The FTC settled in 2006 with no fine. [V]
  - A class action settlement followed (GameSpot, "Take-Two to settle Hot Coffee suits"). The amount was not verified here.
- **Jack Thompson:**
  - He brought lawsuits blaming GTA for real shootings (e.g. on behalf of police victims' families over Vice City).
  - Take-Two pre-emptively sued him in 2007 and they settled.
  - He was disbarred in 2008. [V]
- **GTA IV (29 Apr 2008):**
  - $310M on day 1 and about **$500M / 6M units in week 1** (Take-Two). [V]
  - Budget about $100M (producer's guess; NYT said $80–100M). [S]
  - This launch happened while EA was attempting a hostile takeover of Take-Two (2008). [V]

## 5. GTA Online and recurring money
- **Shark Cards** are real-money packs of in-game cash and the core monetisation. **GTA+** costs $5.99/mo at launch (Mar 2022) and has since been raised. [V]
- **Official numbers:**
  - Take-Two does *not* break out GTA Online revenue.
  - It reported **Recurrent Consumer Spending (RCS) = 78% of FY26 net bookings** ($6.72B). This is company-wide and includes Zynga mobile, so it is not GTA-only.
  - Take-Two expects RCS to be about 65% of FY27 bookings, as GTA VI full-game sales swell the total.
  - Sources: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260521450562/en/Take-Two-Interactive-Software-Inc.-Reports-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2026 and https://gamedev.net/news/nba-2k-zynga-and-gta-drove-take-two-net-bookings-up-19-for-2026-fiscal-year-r3508/ [V]

- **Historic estimates:**
  - "At least $500M" from microtransactions by early 2016 (Benzies lawsuit). [V] that the claim exists.
  - SuperData estimated about **$1.09B** lifetime by mid-2017, and **$521M** in digital console revenue in 2017 alone. https://www.tweaktown.com/news/58516/gta-online-earned-1-09-billion-analyst-firm/index.html [S]

  - About **$9.59M/week ≈ $1.32M/day** from GTA Online, roughly $500M/year.

  - **Over $5B** from Shark Cards between 2014 and 2024.
  - Take-Two stock *rose* after the leak (VGC).
  - Sources: https://kotaku.com/hackers-reveal-rockstar-earns-over-1-million-a-day-from-gta-online-2000687207 ; https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rockstar-hackers-release-their-stolen-data-reveal-that-rockstar-was-right-to-not-pay-them-anything-for-it/ ; https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/rockstar-hackers-actually-made-take-twos-stock-increase-after-leaking-how-much-gta-online-is-making/

## 6. Hook facts
All arithmetic is mine, on the sourced inputs above.

1. **About 54,000 copies a day, 13 years later.** GTA V added about 5M units in Jul–Sep 2026 alone, about 2,260 copies per hour, about 38 per minute. The 13-year average is about 49,000 a day (235M over 4,761 days). [V inputs]
2. **$815.7 million in 24 hours** (Guinness) and **$1B in 3 days**, faster than any film or game ever, including the Avengers film (19 days, per Take-Two). [V]
3. **More copies than people in Brazil.** 235M sold-in vs Brazil's population of about 212M. (TweakTown made a "7th most populous country" comparison at a lower count.) [S] for the population figure; check it before use.
4. **Bought for pocket change.** Take-Two got GTA's publisher for about $14.2M in stock (1998) and its developer for about $11.9M of debt (1999). The franchise has since earned about **$10.6B** in net revenue for Take-Two since 2013 alone. [V/S]
5. **A 2013 game still earns about $2.26M per day.** GTA franchise net revenue in FY26 was about $825M, which is 12.4% of Take-Two. [V]
6. **GTA Online's leaked run-rate is about $55,000 an hour** ($1.32M/day). That is roughly **₹48 lakh/hour** at an illustrative ₹88/$; use the current rate on recording day. [U, leak]
7. **Shark Cards made over $5B** (2014–2024, leak), more than the entire reported $265M budget about 19 times over. [U]
8. **1.9 billion hours** of GTA V streams watched in 2025 is about **217,000 years** of viewing in one year. [S]
9. **Outrage as marketing.** The 1997 original's "moral panic" was deliberately engineered by a tabloid PR man (per its own creators). [V]
10. **A sex mod cost Take-Two $24.5M** in returns (Hot Coffee, 2005). [V]
11. Still the **#20 best-selling game in the US in 2025** by dollar sales, 12 years after launch, and the best-selling PlayStation game ever in the US. [V]

## 7. Disputed / uncertain
- **The $265M GTA V budget** has a single origin (The Scotsman, 2013; attributed in some coverage to art director Aaron Garbut, elsewhere to "estimates"). Rockstar never confirmed it, and the split between development and marketing is unknown. A Guinness snippet shows a $137.5M estimate instead (not fetched). Present it as "reportedly".
- **GTA IV's $100M budget** was a producer's guess, not an audited figure.
- **GTA V lifetime revenue** has no official figure. You may see "$6B" (2018 analyst estimate, before most Online revenue), "$8B+" or "$11B" (fan sites, unreliable). Take-Two *net revenue* for the GTA franchise is about $10.6B (Sep 2013 to Mar 2026), but that figure is derived by TweakTown from percentage disclosures. It includes all GTA products (V, Online, Trilogy etc.) and excludes platform cuts. Say "an estimated ₹X crore" and cite it as an estimate.
- **GTA Online's $5B and $1.32M/day** come from a hacker leak and are unconfirmed. Outlets differ: weekly figures of $9.59M vs $8.5M, Shark Card share of 74% vs 75%, and date ranges of 2013 vs 2014 start.
- **"Sold-in" is not "sold to players."** "Nearly 235M" is Take-Two's shipped-plus-digital count.
- **Franchise units:** 465M, 475M and 480M all appear depending on the date and outlet. "Nearly 475M" was VGChartz from Take-Two data, mid-2026.
- **Guinness "7 records":** Guinness's own news item says 6 *sales* records. The 7th is a trailer record ("most viewed trailer for an action-adventure videogame"). Outlets list the seven differently.
- **Day-1 figure:** Take-Two said $800M (excluding Japan and Brazil, which launched later), and Guinness said $815.7M. Both are fine; cite which one you use.
- **Dates with variants:**
  - DMA founded 1987 vs 1988.
  - GTA 1 PC release 20 Oct vs 28 Nov 1997.
  - Rockstar founded Dec 1998 (formally announced Jan 1999).
  - GTA V Enhanced PC: 4 Mar 2025 (one site says 2024).
- **Max Clifford:** the claim that he engineered the outcry rests on the developers' own accounts. Clifford is a convicted, disgraced figure, so handle with care.
- **GTA III sales of about 15M** is a single source.
- **The Hot Coffee class action payout amount** was not verified.
- **GTA+ current price:** raised from $5.99 (VGC says "up to 40%"). The current regional price was not verified.
- **Not covered here:** GTA VI launch-week numbers. Its 19 Nov 2026 release was three days before this research, and no post-launch sales were found in my searches; other briefs should cover it.
