### 1c. Ten-year outlook (2030–2035)
- No primary firm (Newzoo, PwC) publishes a 2035 number. The credible horizons are **Newzoo ~$234B (2028)** and **PwC ~$300B (2029)** [V]. Extending about 5% a year after that gives roughly $330–400B by 2035. That is **our own extrapolation, not a sourced forecast**. Say "if growth continues at around 5%..." [U].
- Paid market reports quoting $500B+ by 2030 use much wider definitions that include hardware and gambling. Do not use them.

## 4. Hook facts (on-screen ready)
1. **Gaming made $201.6 billion in 2025. That is about 3 times global box office ($33.6B) and recorded music ($31.7B) combined.** [V]
2. **About 3.6 billion people play games**, nearly 1 in 2 humans. Newzoo expects about 4 billion by 2028. [V]
3. **India has 50+ crore gamers, more than the whole US population.** But India's game spending (about $1–1.5B) is **under 1% of the global market**. [V]/[S]
4. **India is #1 in the world for mobile game downloads: 8.45 billion in one year (FY25).** [S]
5. **The EA deal: a Saudi-led group paid $55 billion** for the maker of FIFA/FC, the biggest leveraged buyout ever. It closed in August 2026, and about 20% of the price is debt. [V]
6. **Record revenue, record pain:** about 11,000 game-industry jobs were cut worldwide by October 2026, already more than all of 2025. [S]
7. **GTA 6 set the $80 price norm**, and the industry reshaped its 2026 release calendar around one game. [V]/[S]
8. **One law wiped out about 95% of Dream11's revenue overnight** (Aug 2025). India now bans money games but legally recognises **esports as a sport**. [S]/[V]
9. **A Roblox creator in the top 1,000 averages about $1.3M a year**, but the median creator earns only about $1,440. Game creation pays, but the money is very uneven. [V]
10. **Rockstar paid about $7.9 million in 2019** for the Bengaluru studio (Dhruva) that now works on GTA 6. [V] (Avoid the "1,600 Indians" claim, which Rockstar has disputed.)
11. Free Fire is back: **Free Fire India launched 28 Oct 2026**, four years after the ban. The franchise had about 77M monthly Indian players vs BGMI's about 21M. [S]

## 5. Three viewer takeaways
1. **Build game skills, not just play games.** Learn Unity or Unreal, C++/C#, 3D art (Blender) and technical art. IICT Mumbai (₹400 cr, IIT-style, 17 courses, Google/NVIDIA/Microsoft partners) and global studios in Hyderabad (EA), Bengaluru (Rockstar) and Pune/Mumbai (Ubisoft) are where entry jobs at ₹3–6 LPA start. Programmers and technical artists are paid the most. AI tools are now standard (40% of the industry uses them), so learn to use them.
2. **Esports is now legally a sport in India, but it is not a get-rich-quick route.** The esports market fell about 8% in 2025, and Indus's ₹25 cr tournament stalled. The real money is in the ecosystem around players: casting, team management, event production, analytics, and streaming/content creation on YouTube.
3. **Stay away from "earn money by gaming" apps.** Real-money gaming is now illegal (banks must block it from 1 May 2026). The legal way to earn from games is to **make** them (indie on Steam, Roblox/UGC creation) or **create content** about them. Hyderabad-specific: watch **IndiaJoy (7–13 Dec 2026)** and Telangana's coming **IMAGE 2.0** policy for internships and startup support.
